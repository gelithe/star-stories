# Golden reference · Luis (3–5) — the handcrafted original

**The standard for the fable band.** Small, warm, one clear lesson felt rather
than told. Everything close to home; the "adventure" is a football match. When
the 3–5 prompt changes, generate a Luis and diff against this. If the new one
has a quest, peril, or a companion who transforms, the band has regressed.

## What this book does

1. **The world is the child's own life.** A ball, a garden, a match, teammates,
   the family cat. No magic world — at this age the ordinary IS the adventure.
2. **The tension is one sentence.** He wants to score the most beautiful goal in
   the world — *by himself, all by himself.* Chart-true (Aries Sun) and instantly
   understandable to a four-year-old.
3. **The failure is physical and immediate.** He dribbles one, dribbles two,
   "but three legs are too many, and the ball rolls away."
4. **The feeling is named in the body, simply.** *"In Luis' Bauch steigt eine
   große Welle: erst heiß, dann traurig."* A wave in his tummy: first hot, then
   sad. Two plain words a child owns.
5. **The companion gives the gift in ONE repeatable line, and does not
   transform.** Titty the cat: *"Don't fight the wave. Wait for it. Waves pass —
   and then you see clearly again."* No renaming, no backstory.
6. **The turn is a widening of sight.** After the breath, he sees the whole
   pitch — a friend free on the left, a friend waving on the right. "The game is
   bigger than one player."
7. **The gift is used and it works, twice.** He passes, the friend scores; the
   friend passes back, Luis scores. Joy doubles — shown, not explained.
8. **It ends at home, in bed, with the moral as a whisper** in all three
   languages: *the most beautiful goal is together.*

**Note on "the wave":** this image is not a cliché here — it is Luis's actual
Human Design **emotional authority**, whose own language is waves. It earned its
place in *this* chart. It became a problem only when later generations reused it
for children whose charts never called for it.

## The book

**LUIS ir stebuklingas perdavimas**
*Luis e il passaggio magico · Luis und der Zauberpass*
gimęs po Avino saule, Svarstyklių horizonte
2021 m. balandžio 17 d. · Frankfurt am Main

**1**
Luis pabunda anksčiau už saulę, o jo kojos pabunda dar anksčiau už jį.
Trepu-trepu-trepu — kamuolys jau šoka po stalu, pro duris, per kiemą.
Ant tvoros sėdi katė Titty ir stebi. Katės visada viską mato.

*I piedi di Luis si svegliano prima di lui. Titty osserva tutto.*

**2**
Oggi è il giorno della grande partita. Luis vuole fare il gol più bello del
mondo — da solo, tutto da solo!
Corre come il vento, dribbla uno, dribbla due... ma tre gambe sono troppe, e il
pallone rotola via.

*Luis nori įmušti pats vienas. Bet kamuolys nurieda.*

**3**
In Luis' Bauch steigt eine große Welle: erst heiß, dann traurig. Er setzt sich
ans Spielfeld und schnauft.
Da kommt Titty, setzt sich daneben und sagt leise: „Kämpfe nicht gegen die
Welle. Warte auf sie. Wellen gehen vorbei — und dann siehst du wieder klar."

*Palauk savo bangos, — sako Titty. — Bangos praeina.*

**4**
Luis kvėpuoja. Įkvėpt... iškvėpt. Banga nuslūgsta.
Ir staiga jis pamato tai, ko anksčiau nematė: visą aikštę. Draugą kairėje, kuris
laisvas. Draugą dešinėje, kuris moja. Žaidimas — didesnis už vieną žaidėją.

*All'improvviso Luis vede tutto il campo. Il gioco è più grande di un solo
giocatore.*

**5**
E allora Luis scopre la magia. Non è il tiro. Non è la corsa.
È il passaggio: quando il pallone vola da un amico all'altro, la gioia si
raddoppia. Uno passa, l'altro segna — e il gol è di tutti e due!

**Perduok! · Passa! · Pass!**

*Perdavimas — tai burtai: džiaugsmas padvigubėja.*

**6**
Das Spiel beginnt wieder. Luis läuft, aber jetzt sieht er alles. Er passt den
Ball — sein Freund schießt — TOR!
Und gleich danach passt der Freund zurück, und Luis schießt — noch ein TOR! Die
ganze Mannschaft springt in den Himmel.

*Luis perduoda — draugas įmuša. Draugas perduoda — Luis įmuša!*

**7**
Namuose Lars iškelia Luisą ant pečių, o mažoji Nova ploja delniukais. Katė Titty
murkia — taip katės sako „bravo".
La sera, nel letto, Luis sorride al soffitto.
Und bevor er einschläft, flüstert er das Geheimnis des Tages:

**Gražiausias įvartis — kartu.**
**Il gol più bello è insieme.**
**Das schönste Tor ist gemeinsam.**
