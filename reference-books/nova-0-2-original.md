# Golden reference · Nova (0–2) — the handcrafted original

**The standard for the lullaby band.** A lullaby is not a story with a plot —
it is a small progression of images ending in sleep. When the 0–2 prompt
changes, generate a Nova and diff against this. If the new one has chapters, a
quest, or a lesson, the band has regressed.

## What this book does

1. **Seven tiny spreads, then sleep.** No chapters, no conflict, no moral. It
   opens at arrival ("when Nova was born, the sky smiled") and closes at
   "goodnight, Nova" in all three languages.
2. **One image per spread, said once in each language.** Every spread is a
   two-line couplet, repeated LT → IT → DE. The repetition *is* the lullaby.
3. **Two to six words a line.** Nothing longer than a breath.
4. **Each version re-tells, never translates literally.** "Nova atplaukė iš
   žvaigždžių jūros" / "Nova è arrivata dal mare delle stelle" / "Nova kam aus
   dem Meer der Sterne" — the same image, said the way each language would.
5. **The chart is fully present but invisible.** Pisces rising becomes "she came
   from the sea of stars"; a Cancer Sun in the house of play becomes "her little
   heart is warm like the light of home"; a Scorpio Moon becomes "when the
   feelings are big, the moon rocks her."
6. **The family is named.** Lars and Luis appear as "two bright stars beside
   her" — the real siblings, in the real order.
7. **No companion plot.** The Wood Dragon is noted for the parents; it does not
   walk through the lullaby as a character. Too young for that.
8. **The parents' page carries all the astrology** and the single most useful
   sentence: her feelings don't need fixing, they need keeping.

## The book

**Mažoji žvaigždė NOVA**
*La piccola stella Nova · Der kleine Stern Nova*
gimusi po Vėžio saule ir Skorpiono mėnuliu
2024 m. liepos 16 d. · Frankfurt am Main

**1**
Kai gimė Nova, / dangus nusišypsojo.
Quando è nata Nova, / il cielo ha sorriso.
Als Nova geboren wurde, / lächelte der Himmel.

**2**
Nova atplaukė / iš žvaigždžių jūros.
Nova è arrivata / dal mare delle stelle.
Nova kam / aus dem Meer der Sterne.

**3**
Jos širdelė šilta / kaip namų šviesa.
Il suo cuoricino è caldo / come la luce di casa.
Ihr Herz ist warm / wie das Licht zu Hause.

**4**
Kai jausmai dideli — / mėnulis ją sūpuoja.
Quando i sentimenti sono grandi, / la luna la culla.
Wenn die Gefühle groß sind, / wiegt der Mond sie sanft.

**5**
Lars ir Luis — / dvi šviesios žvaigždės šalia jos.
Lars e Luis — / due stelle luminose accanto a lei.
Lars und Luis — / zwei helle Sterne an ihrer Seite.

**6**
Jos juokas — / mažas saulės spindulys.
La sua risata è / un piccolo raggio di sole.
Ihr Lachen ist / ein kleiner Sonnenstrahl.

**7**
Miegok, mažoji žvaigždute. / Dangus tave laiko.
Dormi, piccola stella. / Il cielo ti tiene.
Schlaf, kleiner Stern. / Der Himmel hält dich.

**Labanakt, Nova. · Buonanotte, Nova. · Gute Nacht, Nova.**
