# Star Stories — the Zodiac Companions

Twelve signature companions, one per Chinese zodiac animal. A child born in the
year of the Rabbit always meets **Mella**; a Dragon child always meets **Vael**.
They are the recurring cast of the whole series — recognisable and re-discoverable
book to book — the way readers know a character across a shelf of stories.

Each companion has a **fixed identity** (name, animal, essence, look) so it stays
itself across books, and a **variable colour**: the year's Chinese *element*
(Wood / Fire / Earth / Metal / Water) tints its mood and palette, so no two
children's versions feel identical. This file is the character bible for
illustration (hand, Higgsfield, or Flux+LoRA) — one trained reference per
companion keeps it consistent.

Names are **never translated** (they are the same in every language of a book).

## The twelve

| Animal | Companion | Essence (element-agnostic) | Signature look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rat | **Pip** | quick, curious, keeper of small shiny things | tiny, bright eyes, a little satchel, one gold whisker |
| Ox | **Bo** | steady, patient, carries the load calmly | broad and gentle, a woven bell, slow warm gaze |
| Tiger | **Zuri** | brave and warm, leaps first | rounded not fierce, a single bold stripe of the year's colour |
| Rabbit | **Mella** | gentle, intuitive, hears feelings early | soft, long-eared, a moon-pale coat, star on the brow |
| Dragon | **Vael** | big-hearted, imaginative, a little wild | small and friendly (not scary), scales that catch light, tiny wings |
| Snake | **Sema** | wise, calm, sees in the dark | slender, coiled cosy, half-lidded knowing eyes |
| Horse | **Rio** | free, roaming, loves the open | a small pony, windswept mane, a gold star hoof-mark |
| Goat | **Fenn** | tender, artistic, a dreamer | soft-curled, carries a little brush or thread |
| Monkey | **Ollo** | playful, clever, turns problems into games | bright, long-tailed, always mid-idea |
| Rooster | **Kesh** | proud, honest, wakes the day | crest like a small sunrise, stands tall and kind |
| Dog | **Baru** | loyal, fair, guards the heart | shaggy, warm, one ear up, a small lantern |
| Pig | **Pim** | generous, warm, grounded | round and content, gold-hoofed, an open honest face |

## How they carry a book

- The companion **arrives near the start**, stays at the hero's side across most
  spreads, and is present at the resolution.
- It embodies the child's **Human Design** mode of acting (a Generator's Pip
  waits to be lit up before it moves; a Projector's Mella guides and is invited).
- It may **earn a fuller name or title** at the story's turning point — and when
  it does, the text gives a short passage on **where that name comes from and
  what it means** (this is the emotional pivot; see the sample "Aquari → Marelli
  → Stellamar", but always explained, not just announced).

## Element tint (the year's Chinese element)

| Element | Palette note |
|---|---|
| Wood | fresh green, new-leaf, growth |
| Fire | warm amber, ember, glow |
| Earth | ochre, clay, grounded gold |
| Metal | silver, cool light, clean edges |
| Water | sea-teal, moon-silver, deep calm |

## Art production

- **Consistency = define once, reference every render.** Build one reference
  sheet per companion (3–4 poses: calm, active, close-up) from the descriptions
  above.
- **Higgsfield / Midjourney:** register each companion as a character / paste the
  sheet as a character reference, then generate every scene against it.
- **Flux + LoRA:** train one small LoRA per companion on its sheet; the
  storefront calls it from the generation pipeline.
- The vector house style already ships a generic `companion` motif; these twelve
  are the upgrade path to a named, recognisable cast.
